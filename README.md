# QRTZ

*Pronounced "quartz."* Live visuals for DJ sets, driven by a small lit crystal on the DJ booth.

A camera locked on the crystal turns its refractions into the visuals on the room's screens. The DJ steers it by spinning the crystal's base, gesturing over it, and through MIDI or Ableton, while tempo comes from the DJ's software over Ableton Link (Rekordbox, Serato DJ Pro, Traktor Pro, Engine DJ, Ableton Live). An LED ring under the crystal matches the screen's palette, so the crowd can see the booth and the screens move together.

**Status:** planning complete, build starting. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for the design, hardware list and milestones.

**Built with:** TouchDesigner, Python, GLSL, Arduino, Ableton Link.
