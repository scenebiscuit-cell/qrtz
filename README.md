# QRTZ

*Pronounced "quartz."* A music visualizer for DJ sets where every image is light through a crystal.

**Standalone:** a virtual crystal rendered in TouchDesigner refracts your photos and paintings. BPM spins it and times the changes, bass pushes the facets, treble makes them sparkle, and drops set off bursts. Tempo comes from the DJ's software over Ableton Link (Rekordbox, Serato DJ Pro, Traktor Pro, Engine DJ, Ableton Live), with beat detection from the audio as the fallback.

**With the crystal:** a small lit crystal on the DJ booth, filmed by a camera, replaces the virtual one. The DJ steers it by spinning its base and gesturing over it, and an LED ring under it matches the screen's palette, so the crowd can see the booth and the screens move together.

**Status:** planning complete, build starting. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for the design, hardware list and milestones.

**Built with:** TouchDesigner, Python, GLSL, Arduino, Ableton Link.
